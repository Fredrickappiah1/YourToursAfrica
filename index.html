About Us

Your Gateway to Authentic African Adventures

Founded in 2022, YourToursAfrica was born from a deep love for the African continent and a desire to share its wonders with the world. We believe travel should transform, inspire, and create lasting memories.

Our team of passionate local experts curates journeys that go beyond typical tourism. We connect you with the real Africa—its people, wildlife, landscapes, and cultures—in ways that are sustainable, respectful, and truly unforgettable.

Capecoast Tours

Known as the heart of Ghana’s historical tourism. This tour typically centers on the Cape Coast Castle (a UNESCO World Heritage site) to learn about the Trans-Atlantic slave trade. It often includes a visit to the nearby Kakum National Park, famous for its thrilling canopy walkway high above the rainforest. As well as the Assin Manso Slave river site.

Accra City Tours

A vibrant exploration of Ghana's capital. Highlights include the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park, the colorful Makola Market, the historic Jamestown district, and Independence Square. It’s the best way to experience the blend of colonial history and modern West African urban life.

Kumasi City Tours

A journey into the "Garden City" and the seat of the Ashanti Empire. You’ll visit the Manhyia Palace Museum, the massive Kejetia Market (one of the largest in West Africa), and nearby craft villages like Bonwire, where the world-famous Kente cloth is woven.
